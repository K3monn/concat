# CONCAT marketing asset kit

## Brand foundation

**Positioning:** A learning platform that helps students build real understanding by teaching what they know, challenging their reasoning, and applying knowledge to something new.

**Primary tagline:** Think for yourself.

**Supporting idea:** Studying is not just about finding the right answer. It is about understanding why it is right.

**Voice:** Human, encouraging, thoughtful, curious, honest, and empowering. Use clear everyday language. Focus on the learner's agency and progress. Ask thoughtful questions. Keep claims specific and credible. AI can be mentioned as part of how the product works, but it is not the brand story.

**Avoid:** fear, shame, exaggerated outcomes, competitive pressure, generic AI imagery, and claims that the product does students' thinking for them.

## Visual direction

- Ink `#0F0E0C`, paper `#F4ECE1`, forest `#2B5940`, warm gray `#E5DDD0`.
- Editorial serif headlines paired with clean sans-serif body copy and occasional handwritten annotations.
- Flat paper-like fields, fine ink rules, small green marks, and simple diagrams of ideas connecting.
- Keep layouts spacious, direct, and human. The student's explanation is the hero.
- The website logo is a dark square with the monospace `[→]` mark, followed by the uppercase `CONCAT` wordmark in Cascadia Code with expanded tracking. Keep the square, arrow, lettering, and proportions together. Do not substitute the older circular `c.` mark.
