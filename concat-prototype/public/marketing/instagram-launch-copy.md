# Concat Instagram launch copy

Use the copy as written or adapt it to your voice. Keep the tone curious, supportive, and honest. Invite people to try the experience; do not promise grades, outcomes, or incentives that have not been confirmed.

## Bio

Learn for understanding, not just the answer.
Teach it. Question it. Apply it.
Student testers + UGC creators: DM “TRY”

## Post 1 — Carousel: Familiar is not the same as understood

**Slide 1**  
You recognize the answer.  
Can you explain why it works?

**Slide 2**  
Notes open: “That makes sense.”

**Slide 3**  
Notes closed: “Wait… how would I say this?”

**Slide 4**  
That pause is useful. It shows you where to get curious.

**Slide 5**  
Teach what you know. Ask why. Try it somewhere new.  
Think for yourself. — Concat

**Caption**  
Sometimes a topic feels familiar because we’ve seen it before. Try closing your notes and explaining the idea in your own words. What comes easily? Where do you want to ask why?

Save this for your next study session, or tell us what you’re learning right now.

## Post 2 — Single image: A study prompt

**Graphic text**  
Before you look at the answer, explain your first step.  
What makes it make sense?

**Caption**  
Try this with one problem today: say what you’d do first, then explain why that step fits. You don’t need perfect words. You’re looking for the part you understand—and the part you want to explore next.

What subject are you working on this week?

## Post 3 — Student tester invitation

**Graphic text**  
Help shape a more thoughtful way to study.  
We’re looking for students to try Concat.

**Caption**  
We’re inviting students to try an early Concat learning experience and share what feels clear, confusing, or useful. Honest feedback is welcome; you don’t need to be a study expert.

Interested? DM **TRY** and we’ll send the details. We’ll explain the time, format, and any terms before you decide.

## Post 4 — UGC creator invitation

**Graphic text**  
Study creators: want to try a different kind of learning app?

**Caption**  
We’re looking to connect with creators who make thoughtful, student-centered study content. We’d love to hear how you’d show a real moment of explaining an idea, getting stuck, or figuring out the why.

Interested in a UGC collaboration? DM **CREATOR** with your profile and a link to a recent example. We’ll share the brief and collaboration terms before anything is made.

## Post 5 — Reel: What if you were the teacher?

**Opening text on screen**  
What if your next study session started with your explanation?

**Shot 1:** Student opens notes, then turns them face down.  
**Voiceover:** “Pick one idea you’ve been studying.”

**Shot 2:** Student explains a simple concept on paper or a whiteboard.  
**Voiceover:** “Try saying it in your own words.”

**Shot 3:** A handwritten “why?” appears beside one step.  
**Voiceover:** “Notice where you want to ask why.”

**Closing text:** Teach. Question. Apply. Reflect.  
**Caption:** Understanding can start with one honest explanation. What would you teach back today?
